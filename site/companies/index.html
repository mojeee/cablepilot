For companies

Electrical departments, panel builders and integrators can try every feature free for 30 days, then license CablePilot per seat, and ask us for help where it matters.

Support

Help with installing, migrating a first project and using the drawing set features.

Training

Hands-on sessions for your team, on your own sample project.

Custom features

Company symbol libraries, title blocks and frames, standard macros for your boards, and data migration from your existing files.

Why it is safe to build on

Public source code you can read and build, an open and documented project format, and no vendor lock-in: your drawings belong to you and stay readable whatever happens to us, also without a licence key.
