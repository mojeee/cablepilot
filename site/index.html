Coming in the next release

An optional assistant with your own API key is planned. It will propose, and you accept or reject. The app is fully usable without it.

Accept Reject

Electrical values the assistant proposes are always labelled as suggestions and must be confirmed by the engineer.

Why CablePilot

Local first

It runs on your computer. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

Open format

Projects are versioned JSON that diffs well in Git. PDF, DXF and CSV or XLSX out. No lock-in.

Sourced values

Every rule and table value cites its standard and clause. What we could not verify is listed, not shipped as fact.

Open source

Apache-2.0. Read it, change it, build on it.
