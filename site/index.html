PLC

Available

PLC racks and I/O cards as devices with addresses from your addressing scheme, an I/O overview that edits both ways, and tag lists for CODESYS and TIA Portal (the TIA format is marked unverified).

Sizing and warehouse

Available

Cables, breakers, contactors and overload relays sized from the schematic with their requirement, assumptions and sources (table values still unverified are flagged), then proposed from your own stock list: ranked by what the warehouse has for this project, with warehouse and location. Confirm one line, or the whole project, in one undo step.

A familiar CAD workspace, with the chat inside

Available

Ribbon, command line with the aliases CAD users type (L, PL, REC, M, CO, TR, Z...), grid, ortho, object snap and polar tracking, window and crossing selection, layers and dockable panels. A chat panel proposes changes as a preview you accept or reject: with your own API key a model reads, checks, sizes and proposes through tools; without a key a built-in assistant answers a few requests.

Cabinet layout

Available

Draw the enclosure, mounting plate, DIN rails and wire ducts with the same CAD tools as the schematic, and place every device of the BOM at its real size, snapped to the rails, with a front view to scale, a side view with the depths, dimensions, no-mount zones and the door devices. Or let CablePilot suggest the whole layout as a preview, with the rules it applied and their sources: it sizes the enclosure with your spare-space percentage, puts power on top, control below and terminals by the cable entry, and keeps what you moved or locked when you suggest again. Layout templates, a documented JSON, CSV and DXF exchange with your ERP, a 3D view, the duct fill rate, wire lengths estimated along the ducts and a drilling DXF come with it.

AI inside, you decide

An optional assistant with your own API key (Claude, OpenAI or Gemini), called from your computer only. It reads the project, runs the checks and the sizing engine with your stock list, and proposes; you accept or reject, and every accepted proposal is one undo step. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

  • Renumber the wires on pages 3 to 8

Why CablePilot

Local first

It runs on your computer. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

Open format

Projects are versioned JSON that diffs well in Git. PDF, DXF and CSV or XLSX out. No lock-in.

Sourced values

Every rule and table value cites its standard and clause. What we could not verify is listed, not shipped as fact.

Open source

Apache-2.0. Read it, change it, build on it.
