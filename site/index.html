A familiar CAD workspace, with the chat inside

Available

Ribbon, command line with the aliases CAD users type (L, PL, REC, M, CO, TR, Z...), grid, ortho, object snap and polar tracking, window and crossing selection, layers and dockable panels. A chat panel proposes changes as a preview you accept or reject: with your own API key a model reads, checks, sizes and proposes through tools; without a key a built-in assistant answers a few requests.

Cabinet layout

Available

Draw the enclosure, mounting plate, DIN rails and wire ducts with the same CAD tools as the schematic, and place every device of the BOM at its real size, snapped to the rails, with a front view to scale, a side view with the depths, dimensions, no-mount zones and the door devices. Or let CablePilot suggest the whole layout as a preview, with the rules it applied and their sources: it sizes the enclosure with your spare-space percentage, puts power on top, control below and terminals by the cable entry, and keeps what you moved or locked when you suggest again. Layout templates, a documented JSON, CSV and DXF exchange with your ERP, a 3D view, the duct fill rate, wire lengths estimated along the ducts and a drilling DXF come with it.

Live link with your ERP

Available

Pull items and stock from your ERP over HTTP (REST or OData v4, with paging), map its fields once, and pick parts from the warehouse straight onto a device. The parts a project needs go back as a documented reservation request that you see before it is sent, by HTTP or as JSON and CSV files in an outbox folder. Credentials stay encrypted on your computer. A demo ERP inside the app shows the whole round trip.

Panel Designer tools

Available

Place a symbol on a wire and the wire splits at its pins. Auto-wire selected symbols in one command. Ladder pages with numbered rungs and line-number references for NFPA 79-style drawings. Project history with daily backups and named versions, restored as one undo step.

How it works

One schematic, everything else follows

  1. 1

    Draw the schematic

    Place IEC symbols, draw wires and give devices their parts. Designations, cross-references and wire numbers follow automatically.

  2. 2

    The lists follow

    Terminal diagrams, cable and wire lists, PLC I/O and the BOM are generated from the schematic and update live. The rule check flags mistakes as you draw.

  3. 3

    Build and hand over

    Lay out the cabinet to scale in 2D and 3D, then print the complete drawing set in your title block, ready for the workshop and the customer’s sign-off.

Optional AI

AI inside, you decide

An optional assistant with your own API key (Claude, OpenAI or Gemini), called from your computer only. It reads the project, runs the checks and the sizing engine with your stock list, and proposes; you accept or reject, and every accepted proposal is one undo step. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Example prompts

  • Add a star-delta starter for a 7.5 kW motor on page 5
  • Renumber the wires on pages 3 to 8
  • Why does the check say Q3 is too small?

A proposal, with a source on every claim

  • A standard and clause
  • A library entry

FAQ

Questions engineers ask

Is CablePilot free? Is it open source?

You can try every feature free for 30 days on each computer, with no account. After the trial, CablePilot needs a licence key, one per user or seat; prices are not set yet, and you can request a key in GitHub Discussions. It is source-available rather than open source: the code is public under the CablePilot License, so you can read it, build it and contribute. The library data (symbols, parts, frames and title blocks) is CC BY 4.0. Your drawings are yours: they stay readable without the app, as documented JSON and as PDF, DXF, CSV and XLSX exports, also after the trial. The optional AI assistant uses your own API key, billed by your provider.

Does it need an internet connection or an account?

No. CablePilot runs on your computer and your projects are files on your disk. Only the optional assistant and the ERP link use the network, and only to the addresses you set.

Which systems does it run on?

macOS on Apple Silicon and Intel, Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), and Linux as an AppImage. The interface and the drawings are in English and Italian.

Can it replace AutoCAD Electrical or EPLAN?

It covers the core work of a panel builder: multi-page schematics with IEC symbols and designations, cross-references, wire numbers, terminal, cable and wire lists, PLC I/O, the BOM, a cabinet layout and the PDF drawing set. It does not import their proprietary project files, and it has no PLC programming or circuit simulation. The comparison above shows what is available today.

Which standards does it follow?

IEC 60617 symbols, IEC 81346 designations, IEC 61082 documents, IEC 60204-1 for machines, IEC 60364-5-52 for cable sizing, IEC 61439 for assemblies, IEC 60445 and IEC 60757 for conductor colours, and ISO 5457 and ISO 7200 for sheets; NFPA 79 and ANSI symbols as an option. Every rule and table value cites its clause, and values not yet verified are listed openly.

Can I use my company’s frame and title block?

Yes. Frames, title blocks with your logo and revision boxes, zone labels and drawing styles are templates, kept in your company library and stored in each project so it opens anywhere.

Does it work with our ERP?

Yes. It pulls items and stock over REST or OData (or from a CSV or XLSX file, or a watched folder), lets you pick parts from the warehouse for a device, and sends the project’s reservation request back by HTTP or to an outbox folder. A demo ERP inside the app shows the round trip.

Is the AI assistant required, and what does it see?

It is optional. With your own key it reads the project to answer; every change it proposes is a preview you accept or reject, every claim shows its source, and electrical values are labelled as suggestions to confirm. Keys are encrypted by your operating system.

Will my files be locked in?

No. A project is one documented JSON file that diffs well in Git, and everything leaves the app as PDF, DXF, CSV or XLSX. DXF pages can be imported too. Your files stay readable after the trial, without a licence key.
